## 2. INTRODUCTION & PRODUCT VISION

### 2.1 Purpose
This PRD defines the problem, scope, requirements, and success criteria for GenericMed. It is the single source of truth for Engineering, Design, QA, Operations, Compliance, and Business teams to build, test, and launch the product consistently.

### 2.2 Product Vision
To make quality generic medicines transparently affordable and trustworthy for every Indian household — by turning price opacity in the pharmacy market into a simple, verified, one-tap comparison and purchase experience.

### 2.3 Product Summary
GenericMed is a SaaS-powered online marketplace that compares real-time prices of generic medicines across licensed pharmacies/suppliers and lets users buy the lowest verified price directly through the platform.

### 2.4 Product Scope
This PRD covers the end-to-end product requirements for the GenericMed platform: patient-facing web/mobile experience, pharmacy/supplier partner portal, prescription verification workflow, price comparison engine, checkout and fulfillment tracking, and admin/analytics systems for the MVP and near-term roadmap.

This PRD does **not** cover:
- Low-level system architecture or infrastructure design
- Source code or detailed API contracts
- Pixel-perfect UI/visual design specifications
- Detailed database schema/implementation (DDL, indexes)
- Final legal/regulatory certification text (to be validated by Legal/Compliance counsel)

## 3. PROBLEM STATEMENT & MARKET CONTEXT

### 3.1 The Problem

**Patients/Caregivers:** Medicine prices for chemically identical generics vary significantly between pharmacies and brands, but patients have no easy way to compare before purchase. This is worse for chronic-illness patients who repeatedly buy the same medicines.

**Doctors/Clinics:** Doctors want to recommend cost-effective generic substitutes but lack a fast, reliable tool to check availability, price, and manufacturer credibility at the point of prescription.

**Small Pharmacies:** Independent pharmacies lack visibility into competitor pricing and demand trends, making inventory and pricing decisions inefficient and often reactive.

### 3.2 Current Problems / Existing Alternatives
- Patients currently rely on walking/calling multiple local pharmacies to compare prices, or trust whatever pharmacy is nearest.
- Some general e-pharmacy apps sell medicines but do not focus on cross-pharmacy generic price comparison.
- Doctors often default to familiar brand names due to lack of quick substitution reference at prescription time.
- Small pharmacies use manual registers/spreadsheets or informal distributor calls to track pricing and stock.

### 3.3 Pain Points

| Stakeholder | Pain Point |
|---|---|
| Patient | Overpays for branded medicine unaware of cheaper generic equivalents |
| Patient | No trust signal for authenticity when buying online |
| Caregiver (chronic care) | Manual reordering every month is tedious; forgets refills |
| Doctor | No fast tool to verify generic substitute availability while prescribing |
| Small Pharmacy | No price intelligence versus competitors; stock-outs or overstock |
| Business | Thin margins on generics require volume — a slow/low-trust product will not scale |

### 3.4 Why Now
India has a large and growing generics ecosystem, rising digital adoption for health services, and increasing consumer price sensitivity in healthcare spending. Government-backed generic initiatives (e.g., Jan Aushadhi-type programs) show established public appetite for verified low-cost generics. This is a directional market signal only — **formal market sizing and demand validation (TAM/SAM/SOM, willingness to pay, adoption rate) is required before scale investment** and is flagged as an open question.

### 3.5 Competitive Landscape
No specific competitor data has been provided, so no competitor facts are fabricated here. Relevant categories of alternatives include:

- **General e-pharmacy apps** (sell branded + generic medicine, but typically single-price-per-SKU, not multi-supplier price comparison)
- **Government generic pharmacy chains** (physical-only, limited digital experience)
- **Local pharmacy aggregator/delivery apps** (hyperlocal delivery focus, not price-comparison focus)
- **Manual/offline comparison** (patient calling multiple pharmacies)

**Expected table stakes:** licensed sourcing, prescription upload, secure payments, delivery tracking, customer support.

**Potential differentiation for GenericMed:**
- Purpose-built **multi-supplier price comparison engine** (not just single-seller catalog)
- Transparent price breakdown building trust
- Doctor-validated substitution suggestions embedded in the buying flow
- B2B angle: pricing intelligence tool for small pharmacies (data flywheel)
- White-label comparison engine for clinics/hospitals

### 3.6 Product Differentiation
Users choose GenericMed because it is the only step where they can see **verified, itemized, lowest-available price for their exact medicine**, backed by authenticity checks and doctor-approved substitution logic — reducing both cost and trust risk in a single flow, which most single-seller e-pharmacy apps do not offer.

## 5. SUCCESS METRICS & KPIs

### 5.1 North Star Metric
**Verified Savings Delivered per Active User per Month** (₹ saved vs. reference/branded price, calculated at checkout).

Rationale: It directly represents the core value proposition (affordability through comparison) and correlates with both user retention and trust, rather than pure transaction volume which could be gamed by low-value orders.

### 5.2 Primary KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Target | Measurement Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly Active Users (MAU) | Unique users completing at least one search/session per month | Growth trend post-launch | Product analytics |
| Search-to-Order Conversion | % of price-comparison searches resulting in a completed order | ≥8% by month 6 | Funnel analytics |
| Prescription Verification Turnaround | Time from upload to verification decision | ≤15 minutes (business hours) | OCR + pharmacist workflow logs |
| Order Fulfillment Success Rate | % of orders delivered without cancellation/failure | ≥95% | Order management system |
| Chronic Refill Retention | % of recurring-delivery users active after 90 days | ≥60% | Subscription/refill module |
| Partner Pharmacy Fill Rate | % of orders successfully fulfilled by assigned partner without stockout | ≥90% | Inventory sync logs |
| Customer Support Resolution Time | Avg time to resolve support ticket | ≤24 hours | Helpdesk system |
| Net Promoter Score (NPS) | User satisfaction/referral likelihood | Baseline in MVP, improve quarterly | In-app survey |

### 5.3 AARRR Framework

| Stage | Definition for GenericMed |
|---|---|
| Acquisition | Organic search (SEO for "generic alternative to [brand]"), doctor referral links, word of mouth |
| Activation | User completes first successful price search AND views a lowest-price result |
| Retention | User returns for repeat purchase or activates a scheduled refill |
| Revenue | Commission/margin earned per completed order |
| Referral | User shares savings/refers another user or doctor shares substitution link |

### 5.4 Guardrail Metrics
Metrics that must **not** deteriorate while optimizing growth:

- Prescription verification error rate (false approval of invalid prescription)
- Medicine authenticity complaint rate
- Payment failure rate
- Refund/return rate
- Delivery delay rate beyond SLA
- Customer data/privacy incident count (target: zero tolerance)
- App/API error rate and latency (see NFR section)

## 7. USER STORIES & JOBS TO BE DONE

### Customer / Caregiver

**Authentication**
- As a patient, I want to sign up/log in using mobile number + OTP, so that I can access my account securely without remembering a password.
- As a returning user, I want to stay logged in on my device, so that I don't need to re-authenticate every visit.

**Discovery**
- As a patient, I want to search a medicine by brand name, salt/composition, or condition, so that I can find it even if I don't know the generic name.
- As a patient, I want to see the lowest price for my medicine across verified sellers, so that I can save money.
- As a patient, I want to see a price breakdown (base price, delivery, tax), so that I trust there are no hidden charges.

**Core Action**
- As a patient, I want to upload my prescription and have it verified quickly, so that I can order prescription medicines without visiting a pharmacy.
- As a patient, I want to see doctor-validated generic substitutes for my prescribed brand, so that I can choose a cheaper equivalent confidently.
- As a patient, I want to verify a medicine's batch/expiry/manufacturer before purchase, so that I trust its authenticity.

**Payment**
- As a patient, I want multiple payment options (UPI, card, wallet, COD where available), so that I can pay conveniently.
- As a patient, I want to be notified immediately if my payment fails, so that I can retry or choose another method.

**Tracking**
- As a patient, I want to track my order status in real time, so that I know when it will arrive.
- As a caregiver, I want to schedule recurring deliveries for chronic medicine, so that I never run out of stock.

**History**
- As a patient, I want to view my order history and total savings, so that I can track how much I've saved over time.
- As a patient, I want to reorder a past order in one click, so that I save time on repeat purchases.

**Support**
- As a patient, I want to reach a pharmacist helpline, so that I can clarify dosage or substitution concerns.
- As a patient, I want to request a return/refund for a damaged or incorrect order, so that I am not stuck with unusable medicine.

### Doctor/Clinic

- As a doctor, I want to search for generic substitutes for a brand I usually prescribe, so that I can recommend a cost-effective alternative.
- As a doctor, I want to share a substitution recommendation link with my patient, so that they can order directly.
- As a clinic administrator, I want to inquire about a white-labeled version of the comparison tool, so that I can offer it under our clinic's brand (future phase).

### Vendor/Partner (Pharmacy/Supplier)

**Onboarding**
- As a pharmacy owner, I want to register and submit my license/documents for verification, so that I can start selling on GenericMed.

**Operations**
- As a pharmacy partner, I want to sync my inventory with the platform, so that customers only see medicines I actually have in stock.
- As a pharmacy partner, I want automated restock alerts, so that I don't run out of high-demand medicines.

**Fulfillment**
- As a pharmacy partner, I want to receive and confirm orders assigned to me, so that I can fulfill them within SLA.
- As a pharmacy partner, I want to mark an order as packed/dispatched, so that the customer sees accurate tracking.

**Earnings**
- As a pharmacy partner, I want to view my payout and order performance reports, so that I can track my earnings and reconcile accounts.

### Admin/Operations

**Management**
- As an admin, I want to approve or reject pharmacy/supplier onboarding applications, so that only licensed sellers are on the platform.
- As an admin, I want to manage medicine catalog entries and mappings (brand ↔ generic ↔ salt), so that search results are accurate.

**Monitoring**
- As an admin, I want to monitor supplier price submissions for anomalies, so that I can detect pricing errors or fraud.
- As an admin, I want to view flagged prescriptions requiring manual pharmacist review, so that compliance is maintained.

**Reporting**
- As an admin, I want demand forecasting reports, so that I can guide partner pharmacies on stocking decisions.
- As an admin, I want compliance and audit reports, so that I can respond to regulatory inquiries.

**Configuration**
- As an admin, I want to configure supported cities, languages, and payment methods, so that rollout can be controlled by market readiness.

### Key Jobs To Be Done
1. "Help me find the cheapest legitimate version of the medicine I need, right now."
2. "Help me never run out of my regular medication without me having to remember."
3. "Help me trust that what I'm buying online is genuine and safe."
4. "Help me (as a doctor) confidently recommend a cheaper alternative without extra research."
5. "Help me (as a small pharmacy) get more orders and not overstock/understock."

- International/cross-border order fulfillment
- Full white-label multi-tenant clinic/hospital platform (roadmap Phase 3+)
- Insurance claim integration
- AI-based clinical diagnosis or dosage recommendation beyond doctor-validated substitution data

*(Clearly labeled as assumptions, not confirmed decisions)*

### 8.4 Constraints

**Business Constraints**
- Must operate exclusively with licensed pharmacies/manufacturers.
- Thin margins on generics require high order volume and negotiated supplier contracts.
- Low CAC required — reliance on organic/SEO/referral channels rather than heavy paid acquisition.
- 24/7 customer support and pharmacist helpline is a mandatory operational commitment from day one.

**Technical Constraints**
- Must integrate with licensed pharmacy/supplier APIs and government drug databases.
- Sub-second search/pricing latency requirement.
- Mobile-first design: PWA + native apps with offline search cache.
- Strict encryption and HIPAA/GDPR-equivalent handling of health data and prescriptions.

**Operational Constraints**
- Requires trained pharmacist staff/on-call network for prescription review and helpline.
- Requires active partner pharmacy inventory accuracy to sustain "real-time" pricing claims.

**Geographic Constraints**
- Initial launch limited to urban and semi-urban Indian cities with sufficient licensed pharmacy density.

**Compliance Constraints**
- Prescription validation, controlled substance exclusion, and pharmacovigilance (adverse event reporting) processes are mandatory before launch — exact legal requirements must be validated with regulatory/legal counsel (flagged in Open Questions).

- Not specified in input; to be defined by Business/Founder (Open Question).

## 10. EDGE CASES & EXCEPTION HANDLING

| Feature | Scenario | Expected System Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Search | Query returns zero matches | Show "No results" state with suggestion to try salt name or contact pharmacist helpline |
| Price Comparison | All suppliers out of stock | Show "Currently unavailable near you" with option to notify user when back in stock |
| Price Comparison | Price changes between search and checkout | Re-validate and prompt user to confirm updated price before payment (FR-CORE-07) |
| Prescription Upload | Blurry/unreadable image | OCR confidence below threshold routes to manual pharmacist review; user notified of delay |
| Prescription Upload | Prescription for controlled/excluded substance | System blocks order, displays policy message, and does not add item to cart |
| Prescription Upload | Expired prescription (per validity rules) | System rejects upload with reason; prompts user to obtain a fresh prescription |
| Authentication | OTP not received | Provide "Resend OTP" after cooldown (e.g., 30s) and alternate login method fallback |
| Authentication | Expired session mid-checkout | Prompt re-authentication; preserve cart contents where technically feasible |
| Payment | Payment gateway timeout | Do not confirm order; check transaction status via gateway API before allowing retry; avoid duplicate charge |
| Payment | Partial payment/refund mismatch | Flag for manual reconciliation by operations; do not auto-close order |
| Order Fulfillment | Assigned partner cannot fulfill after acceptance | Auto-escalate to operations for reassignment (FR-OPS-02); notify customer of any delay |
| Order Fulfillment | Duplicate order submission (double-tap checkout) | System detects duplicate request within short window (e.g., 10s) and prevents double order creation |
| Returns | Return requested for non-returnable item | Block request, display return policy explanation |
| Recurring Delivery | Scheduled refill date falls when item is out of stock | Notify user, offer substitute or delayed fulfillment option, allow manual override |
| Partner Onboarding | Incomplete/invalid documents submitted | Application held in "Incomplete" status with specific missing-item feedback to partner |
| Network | User loses connectivity mid-search (PWA) | Serve cached recent search results (without live price) with an "Offline – prices may be outdated" banner |
| Third-Party Service | Government drug database API unavailable | Fall back to internally cached drug master data; flag authenticity check as "Pending Verification" rather than blocking indefinitely (policy TBD with Compliance) |
| Admin | Two admins attempt conflicting catalog edits simultaneously | Last-write-wins with audit log entry, or optimistic locking conflict warning (implementation decision for Engineering) |

## 11. KEY USER FLOWS

### Primary Customer Flow — Search to Delivery
Start → User searches medicine by name/salt/condition → System returns comparison results with lowest price highlighted → User selects a listing → System checks if prescription required → [If required] User uploads prescription → OCR + pharmacist verification → Approved → User proceeds to cart → User selects delivery address and payment method → System re-validates price/stock → Payment processed → Order confirmed → Order assigned to partner pharmacy → Status updates (packed → shipped → delivered) → User receives delivery confirmation → Completion (order marked delivered, added to history/savings tracker)

### Prescription Rejection Flow (Failure Flow)
Upload Prescription → OCR Extraction → Low Confidence / Invalid Content Detected → Routed to Manual Pharmacist Review → Pharmacist Rejects (reason: unclear/invalid/controlled substance) → User Notified with Reason → User Re-uploads Corrected Prescription → OCR Extraction → Approved → Proceed to Cart

### Payment Failure Flow
Checkout → Payment Attempt → Payment Gateway Declines/Times Out → System Displays Error with Reason → User Chooses Retry Same Method or Alternate Method → Payment Attempt Succeeds → Order Confirmation
*(If retries exhausted: Cart preserved, user directed to support/helpline)*

### Recurring/Scheduled Delivery Flow
User Enables "Schedule Refill" on Chronic Medicine Order → User Sets Frequency (e.g., every 30 days) → System Schedules Next Order → Reminder Sent (Configurable Days Before) → On Scheduled Date: System Re-validates Price/Stock/Prescription Validity → [If Valid] Auto-charge via Saved Payment Method → Order Created → [If Invalid/Stock Issue] Notify User for Manual Action → Completion or Pause

### Admin Flow — Partner Onboarding Approval
Partner Submits Registration + Documents → Application Enters Admin Queue → Admin Reviews License/Compliance Documents → [Approve] Partner Account Activated, Notified, Portal Access Granted → Partner Uploads Inventory/Pricing → Listings Go Live
*(Alternate: [Reject] Partner Notified with Reason → Partner Can Resubmit)*

### Vendor/Partner Flow — Order Fulfillment
Order Assigned to Partner → Partner Notified (SLA Timer Starts) → Partner Accepts Order → Partner Packs Item → Partner Marks Dispatched → Delivery Partner/3PL Picks Up → Customer Tracking Updated → Delivery Confirmed → Payout Calculated in Next Settlement Cycle
*(Failure Branch: Partner Rejects/Timeout → Order Auto-Escalated to Operations → Reassigned to Next Supplier)*

### Operations Flow — Exception Handling
Operations Dashboard Detects SLA Breach (e.g., unaccepted order, delayed dispatch) → Alert Triggered → Operations Investigates → Reassigns Order or Contacts Partner → Resolution Logged → Customer Notified of Status Update if Impacted

### Doctor Flow — Substitution Lookup & Referral
Doctor Logs into Doctor Portal/Tool → Searches Brand Name Prescribed → System Shows Doctor-Validated Generic Substitutes with Price Range → Doctor Selects Substitute to Recommend → Doctor Shares Referral Link/Code with Patient → Patient Opens Link → Pre-filled Search Result Shown → Patient Proceeds to Purchase Flow

## 14. HIGH-LEVEL DATA MODEL

### Core Entities

**User**
- Purpose: Represents a customer/caregiver account.
- Relationships: Has many Addresses, Prescriptions, Orders, Dependents.
- Ownership: Self-owned; caregiver may manage Dependent sub-profiles.
- Lifecycle: Created at signup → Active → Suspended (policy violation) → Deleted (on request, per retention policy).

**Dependent Profile**
- Purpose: Represents a family member managed by a caregiver account.
- Relationships: Belongs to a User; has own Prescriptions and Orders.
- Lifecycle: Created by caregiver → Active → Archived/Removed.

**Medicine (Catalog Entity)**
- Purpose: Master record for a medicine, including brand name(s), generic/salt composition, dosage forms.
- Relationships: Linked to multiple Supplier Listings (price/stock); linked to Substitution Mappings.
- Ownership: Managed by Admin/Catalog team.
- Lifecycle: Draft → Published → Deprecated (if discontinued).

**Supplier/Pharmacy Partner**
- Purpose: Represents a licensed pharmacy or supplier providing inventory and pricing.
- Relationships: Has many Listings (Medicine + Price + Stock); fulfills Orders.
- Ownership: Self-managed via Partner Portal; verified by Admin.
- Lifecycle: Applied → Under Review → Approved/Rejected → Active → Suspended (compliance issue).

**Listing**
- Purpose: A specific supplier's price and stock offer for a given medicine.
- Relationships: Belongs to Supplier; belongs to Medicine.
- Lifecycle: Created/updated by partner → Active → Out of Stock → Removed.

**Prescription**
- Purpose: Digital record of an uploaded prescription requiring verification.
- Relationships: Belongs to User (or Dependent); linked to Order(s).
- Ownership: User-submitted; verified by Admin/Pharmacist.
- Lifecycle: Uploaded → Pending OCR → Pending Manual Review (if flagged) → Approved/Rejected → Expired (per validity rules).

**Order**
- Purpose: Represents a purchase transaction.
- Relationships: Belongs to User; linked to one or more Listings/Suppliers; linked to Payment; may link to Prescription.
- Lifecycle: Created (Cart) → Confirmed → Assigned to Supplier → Packed → Shipped → Delivered / Cancelled / Returned.

**Payment**
- Purpose: Represents a financial transaction for an Order.
- Relationships: Belongs to Order.
- Lifecycle: Initiated → Success/Failed → Refunded (if applicable).

**Scheduled/Recurring Order Plan**
- Purpose: Represents a recurring refill configuration.
- Relationships: Belongs to User; generates future Orders.
- Lifecycle: Created → Active → Paused → Cancelled.

**Substitution Mapping**
- Purpose: Maps a branded medicine to its verified generic equivalents.
- Relationships: Links Medicine ↔ Medicine (brand-to-generic).
- Ownership: Curated/validated by clinical/compliance team.
- Lifecycle: Proposed → Doctor/Clinically Validated → Published.

**Review/Feedback** *(supports trust signals)*
- Purpose: Captures user feedback on order/product experience.
- Relationships: Belongs to User and Order.
- Lifecycle: Submitted → Published/Moderated.

**Audit Log Entry**
- Purpose: Immutable record of sensitive system/user actions.
- Relationships: References any entity (User, Order, Partner, etc.).
- Lifecycle: Created on action → Retained per compliance policy.

### Conceptual Relationships
```
User ─┬─< Dependent Profile
      ├─< Address
      ├─< Prescription ─< Order
      ├─< Order ─< Payment
      └─< Scheduled Order Plan ─< Order (generated)

Supplier Partner ─< Listing >─ Medicine ─< Substitution Mapping >─ Medicine
Order >─ Listing (fulfilled by Supplier)
Any Entity ─< Audit Log Entry
```

| **Phase 3 — Scale & Optimization** | Efficiency, differentiation, expansion | Doctor portal & substitution referral tool, white-label offering for clinics/hospitals, sustainability features (eco-packaging, wastage reduction via demand prediction), advanced pricing anomaly detection, broader geographic rollout | Positive contribution margin sustained; white-label pilot signed with at least one clinic/hospital partner |

**MVP City Rollout:** Pilot in 2–3 initial cities per Assumption A2 before broader urban/semi-urban expansion.

## 20. MVP DEFINITION

> **The MVP is complete when** a patient in a pilot city can search for a medicine by brand/salt/condition, view a transparent, verified lowest-price comparison across at least a minimum viable set of licensed suppliers, upload and get a prescription verified where required, complete a secure payment, and track their order to delivery — with pharmacist helpline support and full compliance/audit logging in place.

### Must Have (P0 — aligned to Section 9 P0 items)
- OTP-based authentication and onboarding
- Search by brand/salt/dosage/condition
- Real-time multi-supplier price comparison with lowest-price highlighting and transparent breakdown
- Doctor-validated substitution suggestions (where available)
- Authenticity checks (batch/expiry/manufacturer)
- Prescription upload, OCR + manual pharmacist verification
- Checkout, multiple payment methods, payment failure handling, digital invoicing, refunds
- Order tracking and status notifications
- Order history and savings tracker
- Returns/refund request workflow
- Pharmacist helpline / 24/7 support access
- Partner onboarding, inventory/pricing sync, order accept/fulfill workflow
- Admin: partner approval, catalog management, prescription review queue, configuration, audit logs
- Core NFRs: security, encryption, sub-second search, availability targets

### Should Have (P1 — fast-follow)
- Scheduled/recurring delivery automation
- COD payment option
- Notification preference management
- Support ticketing system
- Pricing anomaly monitoring
- Automated restocking alerts
- Demand forecasting reports
- Supplier performance scorecards
- Expanded accessibility (voice search, large font)

### Later (P2 — future phase)
- Doctor portal with referral link sharing
- White-label offering for clinics/hospitals
- Sustainability/eco-packaging features
- Advanced competitor pricing insights
- Full multilingual expansion beyond initial set

## 21. RISKS & MITIGATIONS

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Insufficient licensed pharmacy partners at launch, making "lowest price" claim weak or inaccurate | Medium | High | Prioritize supplier acquisition in Phase 0 as a launch gate; set minimum partner threshold per city before go-live |
| Regulatory non-compliance in prescription handling or controlled substance sale | Medium | Critical | Engage legal/regulatory counsel early (Phase 0); build hard system blocks for controlled substances; mandatory pharmacist review workflow |
| Authenticity/counterfeit medicine incident damages trust | Low-Medium | Critical | Enforce batch/expiry/manufacturer verification (FR-CORE-05) as a non-bypassable checkout gate; partner license audits |
| Price staleness due to slow supplier inventory sync causing "lowest price" to be inaccurate at checkout | Medium | High | Enforce checkout-time re-validation (FR-CORE-07); define and monitor sync SLA (NFR-REL-02) |
| Thin margins on generics unsustainable at low order volume | Medium | High | Negotiate volume-based supplier contracts; focus CAC-efficient organic channels; monitor contribution margin KPI (BG-04) |
| Prescription OCR misreads leading to wrong substitution recommendation | Medium | Critical | Mandatory pharmacist sign-off for all prescription-based orders in MVP; do not fully automate approval |
| Elderly users struggle with digital-first UX, limiting adoption of a key target segment | Medium | Medium | Dedicated accessibility features (large font, voice search); consider caregiver-assisted ordering flows |
| Dependency on third-party payment/OCR/government DB APIs causing service disruption | Medium | High | Define fallback behavior (manual review, cached data) per NFR-RES-02; monitor third-party SLAs |
| Data breach of health/prescription data | Low | Critical | Encryption at rest/in transit (NFR-SEC-03), access control (NFR-SEC-02), regular security audits (NFR-SEC-05) |
| Partner pharmacy manipulates pricing to appear as "lowest" fraudulently | Low-Medium | High | Pricing anomaly detection (FR-ADM-04) and admin review before publishing suspicious price changes |
| Slow demand growth due to low brand trust for online generic medicine purchase in semi-urban markets | Medium | High | Doctor referral channel, transparent authenticity messaging, COD option (P1) to reduce purchase friction |
| Scaling infrastructure fails during demand spikes (festivals, disease outbreaks) | Low-Medium | High | Auto-scaling architecture (NFR-SCALE-01), load testing before peak seasons |

## 24. GLOSSARY

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Generic Medicine | A medicine with the same active pharmaceutical ingredient(s), dosage, and intended use as a branded drug, typically sold at lower cost after patent expiry |
| Salt / Composition | The active pharmaceutical ingredient(s) in a medicine, used as the basis for identifying generic equivalents |
| OCR | Optical Character Recognition — technology used to extract text data from uploaded prescription images |
| SKU | Stock Keeping Unit — a unique identifier for a specific medicine/dosage/pack combination sold by a supplier |
| Fill Rate | The percentage of orders a supplier successfully fulfills without stockout or cancellation |
| SLA | Service Level Agreement — a defined performance/response time commitment (e.g., partner order acceptance time) |
| Pharmacovigilance | The process of monitoring and reporting adverse effects or safety concerns related to medicines |
| Controlled Substance | A medicine subject to special legal restrictions on sale/distribution due to abuse potential (excluded from MVP scope) |
| 3PL | Third-Party Logistics — an external logistics provider handling delivery/fulfillment |
| PWA | Progressive Web App — a web application providing app-like experience including offline capability |
| KYC | Know Your Customer — identity/document verification process, applied here to partner pharmacy onboarding |
| NPS | Net Promoter Score — a metric measuring customer satisfaction and likelihood to refer others |
| White-Label | A version of the product rebranded and offered under a partner's (e.g., clinic/hospital) own brand |
| Doctor-Validated Substitution | A generic alternative to a branded medicine that has been reviewed and confirmed appropriate by a qualified clinical reference/process |
